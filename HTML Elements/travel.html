Top 3 Travel Destinations

1. Paris

The city of lights, famous for art, cuisine, and the Eiffel Tower.

“A walk about Paris will provide lessons in history, beauty, and in the point of life.”

2. Kyoto

Historic temples, serene gardens, and traditional tea houses.

“Kyoto is where time flows differently — quiet and contemplative.”

3. New Zealand (South Island)

Dramatic landscapes, fjords, and adventure sports.

“Nature at its grandest — every view is a postcard.”

Travel Tips

  • Pack layers and comfortable shoes.
  • Carry a photocopy of your passport.
  • Learn basic local phrases.
